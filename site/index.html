03 // For bagel shops

A corner, a power outlet, and the ovens on.

You don't need a stage or a back room. If you can fit a small table without blocking the line, you can host.

  • We bringLights and decks. Warm, low lighting, a compact DJ setup and a small speaker. We also bring the crew to set it up and pack it away.
  • You bringA corner and the coffee. You keep running the shop as normal. People come in for the set and stay for breakfast.
  • The soundLo-fi, at talking volume. Slow house, ambient and soul edits. The counter can still hear an order.
  • The timingBefore dawn, through sunrise. We set up while you open and finish while the morning is still quiet. We plan each date with you.
